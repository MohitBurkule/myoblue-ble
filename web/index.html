Connect your MYOblue sensors

  1. Unplug the USB dongle if it is connected to a computer nearby. It grabs the sensors first.
  2. Switch a sensor on. Its light blinks while it waits for a connection.
  3. Tap Add sensor and pick it from the list. Repeat for each sensor.

Works in Chrome on Android and in Chrome or Edge on a computer. On iPhone use the Bluefy browser.

Recordings

    Recordings are saved in this browser as you record, so a crash or closed tab keeps what was captured. Download them to keep a copy.
